A young company with a seasoned team

Core5 was founded in 2022 with a simple premise: businesses shouldn't have to choose between speed and depth when adopting the cloud. We brought together engineers, architects and consultants with decades of hands-on experience at scale — and pointed all of it at helping growing companies transform.

Today we're a Google Cloud Advanced Consulting Partner, working with over 100 customers worldwide across cloud infrastructure, security, data, applications and staffing. We believe in a symbiotic relationship between humans and the future of tech — using automation to remove friction, not people.

Every engagement is backed by a dedicated sales and support team that stays close to the relationship long after go-live.
